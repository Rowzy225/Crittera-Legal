This Privacy Policy explains how Crittera ("Crittera", "the Bot", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the CritterRNG Discord bot. CritterRNG is an independent Discord application and is not operated by or affiliated with Discord Inc.

1. Information Crittera Processes

Crittera may process and store information needed to provide its game features, including:

  • Discord identifiers: Discord user IDs, server/guild IDs, and channel IDs.
  • Game account data: Critter Coins, cooldown timestamps, and gameplay progress.
  • Pet data: owned critters, individual pet IDs, rarity, level, XP, role, element, stats, lock/listing status, equipped-pet status, dungeon status, and event-related identifiers where applicable.
  • Marketplace data: listing IDs, seller IDs, listed pet IDs, prices, and listing timestamps.
  • Dungeon data: dungeon run IDs, participating pet IDs, start times, durations, battle totals, wins, losses, XP totals, run status, and the channel used for completion notifications.
  • Server configuration: configured channel IDs and related settings needed to control where Crittera commands can be used.
  • Interaction data: slash-command inputs and related Discord interaction metadata needed to execute commands.

2. How We Use Information

  • provide and maintain Crittera gameplay;
  • save collections, pets, levels, XP, coins, marketplace listings, and dungeon progress;
  • prevent conflicting actions involving the same pet;
  • enforce cooldowns and game rules;
  • restore eligible game activity after a bot restart;
  • diagnose technical problems and protect the service from abuse; and
  • respond to support, privacy, and deletion requests.

3. User Choice

Server administrators choose whether to install CritterRNG in their servers, and users choose whether to interact with its commands. CritterRNG processes information needed to provide the features users request.

4. Sharing of Information

CritterRNG does not sell personal information. Information may be disclosed when reasonably necessary to operate the service, comply with law, protect users or the service, investigate abuse, or use infrastructure providers that host or support the Bot.

Some gameplay information is intentionally visible to other Discord users as part of the service, such as marketplace seller information, listing details, pet details displayed by commands, and dungeon completion messages.

5. Data Retention

Gameplay and configuration data may be retained for as long as needed to operate CritterRNG, preserve player progress, maintain marketplace and dungeon integrity, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, or satisfy legal obligations. Data may also be deleted during routine maintenance or when a valid deletion request is completed.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and operational measures to protect CritterRNG data. However, no online service or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.
